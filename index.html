About Me

Program: Bachelor of Applied Science in Computer Science (BSCS), Skagit Valley College, Graduating June 2027.
Interests: Software development, Systems and IT infrastructure, Problem Solving, Cyber Security.

I’m a full-time computer science student at Skagit Valley College. I started my career in IT support and PC repair, troubleshooting hardware, Windows Server environments, and help desk issues. I then spent more than a decade in retail management, where I led teams and solved problems under pressure. I returned to college to turn that foundation into a career in software development.

Outside class, I serve as ASSVC President on the Mount Vernon campus, and I mentor first-year BSCS and TRIO students. Leading a team and helping others learn has taught me to communicate clearly and break big problems into manageable steps.

Career goal: To earn an internship or entry-level role in software development or IT, and to keep growing as an engineer who builds useful things and supports the people around me.

Reflection

I chose GitHub Pages because it is free, it keeps my portfolio and my code in one place, and it gives me a link I can share with employers and update at any time. I was already using GitHub for my class projects, so it was the natural choice.

The project that best represents my abilities is pull-request-lab-template. In it, I forked my instructor’s template repository, made changes on my own copy, and submitted them as a pull request. Once the changes were approved, they merged into the main branch. This is the workflow software teams use every day, and it showed me that I can contribute to a shared codebase without breaking it.

In the future, I want to add more projects with screenshots and short demos, write clearer explanations of the decisions I made, and add a contact form. I also want to build larger projects that connect a front end, a back end, and a database.

This portfolio supports my career goals by giving employers evidence of what I can do, not only what my resume says. Together with my leadership and mentoring experience, it shows that I can learn new tools, work with a team, and keep improving.
